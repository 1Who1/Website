The Gallery was all started by Carter Angelo when he wanted to start presenting his art to the public and get recognition from it as well as get people to buy his artworks from him, this gallery is all on Carter Angelo and his artworks to show the world his artistic skills.

Carter Angelo is a proud owner of a local Gallery based in Northampton, he has taken art as a professional hobby for over 20 years this website was made to showcase Carter Angelos artworks online and spread his name and allow everyone to be able to view his artworks whether they are local or not.
